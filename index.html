About BrewHaus

We started BrewHaus because we were tired of choosing between good coffee and knowing where it came from. Every bag we sell lists its origin, altitude, and roast date — not a "best before" stamp, an actual date.

We also sell the gear worth owning: drippers, grinders, and kettles we've tested ourselves, not just the ones with the best margins.
